## Continuous script (one-take TTS/VO)

Meet the Representative Location Explorer — the tool that decides where to simulate America's building stock, and shows its work. It's built for the people who model the nation: energy and climate modelers, researchers, urban planners, and the policy makers who act on their results. It turns a fuzzy judgment call into one defensible, repeatable answer. Here's the challenge. You can't simulate every community, and the geography is full of traps — a ZIP code is not a county, and the wrong filter quietly biases everything downstream. So the Explorer selects twenty representative catchments, one for every combination of five climate regions and four urbanicity classes, covering the country's housing and population diversity. Open the dashboard and the whole strategy is there at a glance: every stratum, its chosen location, the candidates considered, and which simulation filters are verified. Start from anything — even a ZIP code. The Explorer resolves it to the real Census geography — ZCTA, county, and metro — with its uncertainty, and never pretends a ZIP is a boundary it isn't. Then make it yours: adjust the density screen, the scoring weights, and the coverage rules, and a deterministic allocation re-solves instantly, fully reproducible and exportable as versioned files. And every trade-off is measured — protecting national coverage here keeps over ninety-nine percent of the score, and each substitution shows exactly what it cost and why. That's the whole point: a method that's transparent by design — deterministic, reproducible, and auditable, with exact simulation filters anyone can check. Representative Location Explorer: rigorous method, made inspectable. Try the live demo at representative-location-explorer.onrender.com.

## Better voice — two free paths

1. **In-browser (fastest):** open `promo.html`, click play, and pick a ★ premium voice
   from the top-right menu. On macOS install one once via
   *System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System Voice → Manage Voices →
   English → "Ava (Premium)"* (or a Siri voice). Then screen-record the window
   (QuickTime → New Screen Recording, capture system audio) for a voiced MP4.
2. **Studio (best):** paste the continuous script into ElevenLabs (free tier), export
   MP3, and overlay it on `promo.mp4` in any editor — the timings above are aligned.
