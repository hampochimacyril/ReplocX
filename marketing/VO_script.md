## Continuous script (for one-take TTS/VO)

Meet the Representative Location Explorer — a decision-support tool for choosing where to simulate America's building stock. National building-stock and heat-health studies can't model everywhere, so which handful of places genuinely represent the whole country? The Explorer selects twenty representative catchments — one for every combination of five climate regions and four urbanicity classes. Search by ZIP code, and it resolves the real geography — ZCTA, county, and metro — without ever blurring a ZIP into a boundary it isn't. Adjust the density screen, the score weights, and the coverage rules, and watch a deterministic allocation update in real time. Every trade-off is measured: protecting national coverage retains over ninety-nine percent of the unconstrained score, and each substitution shows exactly why. It's transparent by design — deterministic, reproducible, and fully auditable, with exact simulation filters and versioned, exportable scenarios. Representative Location Explorer: rigorous method, made inspectable. Explore it live.
